Going Digital — Committee Briefing & Questions
Prepared for the Managing / Executive Committee  •  24 July 2026  •  For discussion & approval

What this is: We are upgrading Samarpan's website so that membership and event work that is done today on paper becomes digital — with almost nothing for the committee to maintain and strong protection for members' personal information. This page explains the plan in plain terms and lists the few things we need the committee to decide so we can begin.

What will change — for members and for the committee

For a member (on their phone)

  • Fill the membership form online (same form as today, in Gujarati & English) and upload photos.
  • Pay the fee through a secure Kuwait payment link (MyFatoorah).
  • Receive a digital membership ID card with a QR code by email — no more waiting for a printed card.

For the committee

  • All applications arrive automatically in one secure list (replaces the paper forms and the Excel file).
  • Approve a member with one click; the ID card is issued and emailed for you.
  • At Navratri, guest passes are sold online and checked by scanning the QR at the gate.

What it costs & how it stays safe

  • Cost: effectively free. The website and the system run on free, trusted services (Google + standard web hosting). The only running cost is MyFatoorah's small fee on each online payment, and possibly ~KD 0.75/month later if we need extra photo storage.
  • Security: member details and photos are held in the association's own Google account, visible only to the few committee members you choose. There is no website database for hackers to break into, and nothing that needs a technical person to keep updated.
